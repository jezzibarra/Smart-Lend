1. Overview & Business Scope

The Smart Lending Application (SLAP) is a micro-lending software platform created to help small lending enterprises manage customer accounts, compute loan amortization schedules, and reconcile digital payments securely.

We take the privacy and confidentiality of financial information seriously. This policy outlines what data we collect, why we collect it, how cookies and local browser storage are utilized, and how we protect your records.

2. Information Collected

We only collect data strictly necessary to fulfill loan administration, payment reconciliation, and authentication services:

  • Borrower Profile Data: Full legal name, contact telephone number, and Gmail address used during account registration.
  • Loan & Financial Records: Loan principal amounts (₱), agreed interest rates, loan terms (months), and current loan standing (Active, Fully Paid, or Overdue).
  • Payment Verification Tokens: E-wallet transaction reference numbers (such as GCash and Maya reference codes), recorded timestamps, and payment channels.
  • Authentication Credentials: Secure Google OAuth verification tokens and encrypted administrator session tokens. We never store physical credit card PINs or e-wallet account passwords.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

SLAP utilizes lightweight cookies and HTML5 localStorage to maintain authenticated sessions and remember user preferences. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies.

Cookie / Storage Key Type Purpose Duration
slap_cookie_consent Local / Cookie Stores your cookie consent preference (Accepted / Essential). 1 Year
adminUser LocalStorage Maintains active administrative staff session and role-based permissions. Session / Logout
clientUser LocalStorage Maintains authenticated borrower login state. Session / Logout
g_state Cookie Set by Google Identity Services for authorized OAuth sign-in. Google Standard
Cookie Control: You can clear your cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out of your current session.

4. How We Protect Your Financial Data

Your financial records are safeguarded using multiple security controls:

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative ledger operations, borrower registries, and transaction logs are accessible exclusively by verified administrative users.
  • One-Time Password (OTP) Verification: Borrower accounts must verify their registered Gmail address via a time-sensitive OTP code before applying.
  • Whitelisted Administrative Access: Google Sign-In for administrators enforces an authorized email whitelist before granting dashboard permissions.

5. Your Rights as a Borrower

As a borrower registered in the SLAP platform, you have the right to:

  • Inspect your active loan balances, installment schedule, and recorded payments.
  • Request correction of inaccurate contact numbers or borrower details.
  • Receive a clean status confirmation once your loan balance is fully paid.
